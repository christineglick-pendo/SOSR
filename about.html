About This Site

This boilerplate exists purely for testing: tag managers, analytics, form flows, and mock authentication UI, without any real backend.

What's included

  • Five static pages linked via a shared top navigation
  • A handful of placeholder images (see the Gallery tab)
  • A contact form with an email field on the Contact tab
  • A mock Sign In modal available from every page
  • Google Tag Manager installed on every page

No backend

Every interaction here is handled client-side with plain JavaScript. There is nothing to configure or connect.

Not a SPA

Each tab is a real page load, which keeps things simple for tools that expect traditional page navigation.
