Welcome to the Test Boilerplate Site

This is a simple multi-page site for QA and tooling tests: tabs, images, a checkout form, and a mock sign-in flow.

Multi-page

Every tab is its own HTML page (no client-side routing), so page loads and navigation behave like a traditional site.

Mock Sign-In

The Sign In button opens a modal that accepts any email address and password. Nothing is validated, stored, or sent anywhere.

Checkout Form

The Checkout page is a mock cart with email and credit card fields (fake numbers only). Submitting shows an order confirmation; no data is sent to a backend.
